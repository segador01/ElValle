El Valle

What is it?

El Valle is the name of the lovely area where I live with my family. That's Spanish for 'The Valley.' It's located in a pretty remote and quiet part of the country.

Towns

A handful of beautiful villages with colourful names can be found here, including:

  • Rebollar
  • Rollamienta
  • Valdeavellano de Tera
  • Villar del Ala
